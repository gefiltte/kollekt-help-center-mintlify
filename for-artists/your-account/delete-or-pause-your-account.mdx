title: "Delete your account"
description: "Deleting your Kollekt account is permanent. Here's where to find the steps."
sidebarTitle: "Delete account"
---

Deleting your account removes it for good, and it can't be undone. If you own an artist's Kollekt or are on someone's team, talk to us first at [support@kollekt.io](mailto:support@kollekt.io), so the artist's Kollekt and its earnings stay in the right hands.
