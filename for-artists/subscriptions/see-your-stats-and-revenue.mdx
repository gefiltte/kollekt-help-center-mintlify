Stats is the one place to answer "how's my space doing?". Earnings, fans, subscribers, Direct Line and Chat activity, and a full list of payments, all filtered by date range. Numbers only, no fluff.

## The Stats overview

Open Stats from the Settings icon in the bottom nav, then tap **Stats**.

![Stats overview screen](/for-artists/admin/screenshots/admin-stats-overview.png)

The stats card covers:

- **Earnings.** Total money made in the selected range.
- **Members.** How many fans you have right now, plus **New** and **Unfollowed** in the range.
- **Subscribers.** How many subscribers right now, plus **New** and **Cancelled** in the range.
- **Direct Line Messages.** How many you posted in the range.
- **Chat Messages.** How many were posted in the range.
- **Transactions.** Each payment from a fan in the range.

## Pick a date range

Everything on Stats is filtered by date range. The pill near the top controls what the whole screen shows. Tap the date range pill to open the calendar.

![Calendar date picker](/for-artists/admin/screenshots/admin-stats-date-picker.png)

Pick a start and end date, then tap the blue checkmark. The Stats screen updates right away.

## What to check and when

- **Weekly.** Glance at new members and new subscribers. Are you growing?
- **After a release or big post.** Change the date range to the post day and the two days after. Direct Line Messages up, members up, subscribers up: you just learned what works.
- **Monthly.** Earnings total and transaction count. Match what you expected? If not, something's off and worth flagging.
