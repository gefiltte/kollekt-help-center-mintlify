---
title: "Add team members and split revenue"
description: "Share your earnings with your manager, label or band. Only the owner sets the split."
sidebarTitle: "Team and splits"
---

A split shares your earnings with the people who work on your Kollekt, such as your manager or label. The owner adds each person and their share under Payments, and whatever is left goes to the owner automatically. Everyone in the split then sets up payouts for their own bank account.
