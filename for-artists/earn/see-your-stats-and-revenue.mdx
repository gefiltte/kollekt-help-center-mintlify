Stats show how your Kollekt is doing over a period you choose: what members paid, how many members and subscribers you have, how much people talk, and every payment. The owner and admins can see them.

## Finding your stats

On your Kollekt, tap your picture with the gear in the tab bar, then **Stats**. Stats open on the last 30 days. The calendar at the top lets you pick any **Start date** and **End date**, and every number on the screen follows that period.

[Step-by-step: How do I read my stats?](https://help.kollekt.io/en/article/how-do-i-read-my-stats-17n283x/)

## What the numbers show

|  | What it counts |
| --- | --- |
| Earnings | What members paid in the period, after VAT and payment fees |
| Members | Everyone who has joined, and how many joined in the period |
| Subscribers | Everyone subscribed now, with new and cancelled subscriptions in the period |
| Direct Line messages | Posts on your Direct Line in the period |
| Chat messages | Messages in your chats in the period |

Under **Transactions** you see each payment in the period: the date, the member's email, whether it was a subscription or a drop, and the amount.

<Note>
  Earnings on Stats are counted before Kollekt's 20 % commission and before your team's split. What each person actually receives is under **Payments** in the admin menu, as Pending and Paid.
</Note>

## Ways to use your stats

Every Kollekt grows differently, so there's no number to aim for. Some ways artists use their stats:

- **A quick look now and then** at new members and subscribers, to see how things are moving.
- **After an announcement, a drop or a show.** A date range covering that day and the days after shows how many people joined or subscribed around it.
- **Comparing periods,** like this month and last month, or a tour and the weeks before it.
- **A whole year at once.** Setting the dates to a full year shows that year's earnings, which can help with taxes and bookkeeping.
