Payouts on Kollekt run through Stripe. Connecting takes a few minutes and needs your bank details, a bit of ID, and your tax info. You can do this any time. Your earnings start adding up from the first sale, and connecting is only needed before you want to take the money out.

Only the page owner sets this up. Team members on a revenue split set up their own payout under their own profile.

## Open the payout form

1. Tap your user profile icon at the top of your Home page.
2. Tap the gear icon with **Edit** to open your user profile editor.
3. Scroll to the bottom. You'll see a **Set up payouts** section with a **Set up** button.
4. Tap **Set up**.

![Edit profile showing Set up payouts](/for-artists/your-account/screenshots/userprofile-edit-bottom.png)

## Fill in bank details and identity

![Payout form with bank details and identity fields](/for-artists/your-account/screenshots/userprofile-payouts-1.png)

Two sections open up:

- **Bank details.** Account Holder Name, Bank Country, Account Number or IBAN.
- **Identity.** Legal First Name, Legal Last Name, Date of Birth.

All fields are needed. A red message shows under any empty field when you try to save.

## Fill in address and tax info

![Payout form with address and tax info](/for-artists/your-account/screenshots/userprofile-payouts-2.png)

- **Address.** Street Address, City, Postal Code, Country.
- **Tax information.** Tax Identification Number (TIN), TIN Country, VAT ID (optional).

Tap **Save** at the bottom of the form.

## What happens after

Your payout account is connected. Once connected, earnings pay out on Stripe's normal schedule for your country. Any money held before you connected comes through on that same schedule.
