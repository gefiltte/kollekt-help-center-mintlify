title: "Connect your payout account"
description: "Add your bank details once in your own profile, and your earnings are paid out every month."
sidebarTitle: "Get paid"
---

Payouts send what you earn on Kollekt to your bank account. You set them up once, in your own profile, with your bank, identity and tax details. Payouts go out at the start of each month.

Everyone in your earnings split, not just the owner, sets up payouts for their own bank account.
