Right after claiming, your Kollekt is open: people can already join and subscribe. A few small touches in the first minutes make it feel like yours when they arrive.

## A few good places to start

**Your name, cover and bio.** These are the first things people see. A quick look is enough to check they feel right. See [Edit your Artist page](/for-artists/your-space/editing-the-artist-page).

**A first Direct Line post.** A short hello, a photo or a voice note means the first people who join find something waiting. Every member gets Direct Line posts, so it's also a nice way to see how the Direct Line works. See [Send a Direct Line message](/for-artists/your-space/sending-messages).

**Your Kollekt link in your bios.** Instagram, TikTok or wherever your audience already is. A link in a bio keeps working long after a single post is gone. See [Add Kollekt to your social bios](/for-artists/bring-fans-in/social-bios).

## Ready to tell people?

[Announce Kollekt to your audience](/for-artists/bring-fans-in/announce-to-your-fans) has ideas for where to share it, and words you can borrow.

## When you have more time

- [Add and organize link groups](/for-artists/your-space/add-and-organize-link-groups). Room for more links beyond your social icons, such as tour dates, new music or merch.
- [How subscriptions and earnings work](/for-artists/earn/how-subscriptions-and-earnings-work). What subscribers get, and how the money works.
- [Connect your payout account](/for-artists/earn/connect-your-payout-account). Bank details in your own profile, so your earnings can be paid out at the start of each month.
- [Add team members and split revenue](/for-artists/earn/team-members-and-revenue-splits). For bands, managers, labels or anyone else who helps run your Kollekt.
