The three highest-leverage moves to do once your page is claimed. Check your profile, send a test Direct Line message, and add Kollekt to your social bios. Together they take about 10 minutes and make your space ready for fans.

## The three moves

1. **Check your cover photo and artist name.** Both come already filled in. Update them if anything's out of date or wrong. See [Edit your Artist page](/for-artists/your-space/editing-the-artist-page).
2. **Send one Direct Line message to yourself.** Post a photo and a sentence to see how it reaches fans. See [Send a Direct Line message](/for-artists/your-space/sending-messages).
3. **Copy your Kollekt link and add it to your social bios.** Instagram, TikTok, wherever your audience hangs out. This is the most important place to put your link. See [Add Kollekt to your social bios](/for-artists/bring-fans-in/social-bios).

## Ready to invite fans?

Once your page is set up, go to [Announce Kollekt to your fans](/for-artists/bring-fans-in/announce-to-your-fans) for words you can copy and where to post them.

## What you can do later

Once the basics above are in, here's what to set up next when you're ready:

- [Add and organize link groups](/for-artists/your-space/add-and-organize-link-groups). Add other links beyond your social icons (tour dates, releases, merch).
- [How subscriptions and earnings work](/for-artists/earn/how-subscriptions-and-earnings-work). Plan what to post just for paying fans, and how the money works.
- [Connect your payout account](/for-artists/earn/connect-your-payout-account). Set this up before Stripe can pay out what you've earned.
- [Add team members and split revenue](/for-artists/earn/team-members-and-revenue-splits). Bring in your manager, label, or video team.
