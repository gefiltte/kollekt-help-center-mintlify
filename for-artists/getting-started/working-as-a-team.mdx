A Kollekt doesn't have to be run by one person. A band can share it, a manager can look after posts and drops, and a label can work across several artists. Everyone uses their own account, so nobody needs to share a password.

## Roles

Every Kollekt has one **owner**, and can have as many **admins** and **moderators** as it needs.

|  | Owner | Admin | Moderator |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Keep the chats friendly (remove messages, timeouts, slow mode) | ✓ | ✓ | ✓ |
| A chat of their own with the rest of the team | ✓ | ✓ | ✓ |
| Posts, drops, products, chats, groups and gifts | ✓ | ✓ | · |
| Post as the artist on the Direct Line and in chats | ✓ | ✓ | · |
| Stats and the member list | ✓ | ✓ | · |
| Add and remove admins and moderators | ✓ | ✓ | · |
| Earnings splits | ✓ | · | · |

Someone needs to have joined your Kollekt before they can get a role. The owner and admins add people under **Roles** in the admin menu, and a member can also be made a moderator straight from the chat.

To change who owns a Kollekt, email [support@kollekt.io](mailto:support@kollekt.io).

## Sharing earnings

The owner can split earnings with the team: a manager, the other band members, a label or anyone else. Each person gets a percentage, and what's left goes to the owner. A product, like one drop, can also have a split of its own.

Everyone in the split sets up payouts once, in their own profile, and is paid their own share at the start of each month. Until they do, their share waits for them.

## Ways teams work

There's no single way to do it. Some setups artists use:

- **A band,** where one member is the owner and the others are admins, so everyone can post and drop into the chats, and the earnings are split between everyone.
- **An artist and a manager,** where the manager plans drops and schedules posts, and the artist drops into the chats and sends voice notes.
- **A label or management company,** with one person on the team of several artists, as the owner of some and an admin of others.
- **Trusted members as moderators,** so the team can focus on posting while the chats stay friendly.

Your team also has two chats of its own: **Admins** for the owner and admins, and **Moderators** for moderators and admins. They're a place to plan, agree on how to handle something, or just check in.

## Working with more than one artist

One account can be on the team of several artists. Each artist has their own admin menu, and the artists you've joined are listed under **Artists** in the menu.

Some things are shared across all of them:

- **One profile and one set of bank details,** so payouts from every artist go to the same account.
- **One Claude connector link,** which covers every Kollekt you own or are an admin of, including ones you join later.

## Tools for teams

- **Groups** let you send posts to some of your members, give them their own chat or a badge. Groups are chosen by country and join date.
- **The member list** can be searched and downloaded as a CSV file.
- **Ad pixels** for Meta, Google Ads and TikTok show your ad platform who visits, opens the subscribe screen and pays.
- **Claude** can read your members, chats, stats and earnings and answer questions about them in your own words. It can only read, never change anything.
