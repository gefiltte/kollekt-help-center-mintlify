Every space starts with two rooms: **Main Chat** for every member, and **Subscribers Chat** only for paying subscribers. Fans talk to each other and to you. Anyone can send a message, tap a heart to react, or reply to someone. Replies show up under the original message.

To remove messages or put a member in timeout, see [Moderate your chat](/for-artists/your-space/moderate-your-chat).

![Chat with active community conversation](screenshots/surface-chat.png)

## Use it

- [Run your community chat](/for-artists/your-space/community-chat). Switch rooms, post as your artist or as yourself.
- [Moderate your chat](/for-artists/your-space/moderate-your-chat). Put members in timeout, make members moderators.
