Chat is the group room inside your Kollekt space, where fans talk to each other and to you. Every space has two rooms: **Main Chat** is open to every member, and **Subscribers Chat** is only for paying subscribers. Anyone can post, react, and reply. Replies start a thread under the original message.

For moderation actions, see [Moderate your chat](/for-artists/your-space/moderate-your-chat).

![Chat with active community conversation](screenshots/surface-chat.png)

## Use it

- [Run your community chat](/for-artists/your-space/community-chat). Switch rooms, post as artist or personal.
- [Moderate your chat](/for-artists/your-space/moderate-your-chat). Timeouts, promote moderators.
