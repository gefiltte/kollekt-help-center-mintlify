title: "Add and organize link groups"
description: "Group your links into sections on your Home, such as Music, Tour or Merch."
sidebarTitle: "Link groups"
---

Link groups put your links into sections on your Home, so members find your music, tour dates and merch in one tap. You add and arrange them in the same place where you edit your name, bio and cover.
