Direct Line is your broadcast feed inside Kollekt. Every post sends a push notification to every fan who's joined your space, so messages always reach them. No algorithm, no feed competition. Voice notes, photos, videos, and text all work.

It's the highest-reach surface on your page. Use it like dropping a message to a close group of friends: short, frequent, real.

![Direct Line feed with posts from the artist](screenshots/surface-direct-line.png)

To lock a post so only paying subscribers can open it, see [Post subscriber-only content](/for-artists/your-space/post-subscriber-only-content).

## Use it

- [Send a Direct Line message](/for-artists/your-space/sending-messages). Text, voice, photos, videos, and audio.
- [Post subscriber-only content](/for-artists/your-space/post-subscriber-only-content). Locked posts for paying fans.
