A drop is something you release on your Kollekt, with posts gathered around it. It can be as small as one demo or as big as an album launch, and it can be free or have a price. When you have a drop out, a **Drops** tab appears on your Kollekt.

## What a drop can hold

A drop has a cover, a name and a description, and up to 8 extra pictures or videos. Inside it are its posts: anything from photos and videos to voice notes and words. Posts in a drop can also carry:

- **A vote,** where members pick an option before a deadline, and the post shows the winner when it closes.
- **A raffle,** where members enter with one tap and up to 20 winners are picked.
- **A link,** for example to tickets or a shop.

Posts can be scheduled. A post on its way shows a countdown, like "3 days", with its title hidden until it opens, so a drop can unfold over days or weeks.

## Free or paid

A drop can be **free**: members join it with one tap. Or it can have a **price**: members buy it once, and pay what they want from the minimum you set. You choose a suggested price, a minimum and the currency (EUR, USD or GBP).

A drop is separate from a subscription. Buying a drop doesn't make someone a subscriber, and a subscription doesn't open a drop. Kollekt's commission on drops is the same 20 %, counted after VAT and payment fees.

## Ideas for drops

There's no right kind of drop. Some ideas artists use:

- **Before a release.** Demos, the story behind a song, a vote on the single's artwork, then the release itself.
- **Around a tour.** A raffle for tickets or a meet and greet, then photos and voice notes from the road.
- **A presale or early access** to tickets or merch, through a link inside the drop.
- **Unreleased things.** Live recordings, B-sides, old demos or a stripped-back version of a song.
- **Behind the scenes.** A studio diary over a few weeks, with a new post on its way every few days.
- **Something small and free,** like a single demo or a thank-you, as a first taste of what drops can be.

For a band, each member might add their own posts to the same drop. For a manager or a label, a drop can be a way to plan a release campaign with the artist, with posts scheduled ahead.

## A few things worth knowing

- **A drop starts as a draft.** Only your team sees it until you switch it to live.
- **A drop can be hidden again** by switching Live off. Drops can't be deleted, but a hidden drop disappears from your Drops tab, and you can switch it back on later.
- **A drop helps your leaderboard.** When someone a member brought in gets a drop for the first time, it counts as that member's referral.
- **Your Stats show drop sales** under Transactions, next to subscriptions.
