## Three main roles in your team

Open Roles from the Settings gear in the bottom nav. Three sections stack one under the other:

- **Owner.** The artist who claimed the page. Can do everything, including Payments and revenue splits.
- **Admins.** Can open Stats and Roles. Can't change Payments or revenue splits. Empty until you add someone.
- **Moderators.** Can moderate Chat: timeout members, make them moderators, remove messages. Empty until you add someone. See [Moderate your chat](/for-artists/your-space/moderate-your-chat).

![Roles screen with Owner, Admins, and Moderators](screenshots/admin-roles-screen.png)

## Add an admin or a moderator

1. Tap **Add admin** or **Add moderator**.
2. Type a name or username in the search field.
3. Tap a result to add them.

![Moderator added](screenshots/admin-roles-moderator-added.png)

A green confirmation banner appears.

## Remove an admin or a moderator

Tap the delete icon next to the row. **Remove** appears. A green banner confirms the removal.
