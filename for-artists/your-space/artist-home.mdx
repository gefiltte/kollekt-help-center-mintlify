Your Kollekt is your own place on Kollekt: your picture and links, the posts you send to everyone who joined, and the chats where members talk. People move around it with the tab bar at the bottom of the screen.

| Tab | What's there |
| --- | --- |
| Home | Your picture, name, bio, social links and the links you're sharing right now. |
| Drops | Your drops. The tab shows while you have a drop out. |
| Direct Line | Your posts to everyone who joined. |
| Chats | Where members talk with each other and with you. |

For the owner and admins, the tab bar also has your picture with a small gear. That's your admin menu.

## Home

Home is what people see when they open your link. It has your cover picture, name and bio, your social links and your link groups, for things like new music, show tickets or merch.

The share button in the top left gives a link to your Kollekt, and people who aren't members yet can join from the bottom of the screen.

## Drops

A drop is a post built around something you offer: a demo, a ticket presale, a signed poster or anything else. A drop can be free or have a price, and the Drops tab gathers them in one place.

See [Step-by-step: How do I create a drop?](https://help.kollekt.io/en/article/how-do-i-create-a-drop-1f36eyt/)

## Direct Line

The Direct Line is where you post to everyone who joined: words, photos, videos, links, voice notes, polls and raffles. Members get a push notification, or an email if a push can't reach them, so a post doesn't depend on an algorithm to be seen.

A post can also be kept for subscribers, sent to one group of members, or scheduled for later.

## Chats

Every Kollekt starts with two chats: **Main Chat** for every member, and **Subscribers Chat**, which only subscribers see. Members can write, reply and heart messages. You and your team can add more chats, and moderators help keep them friendly.

## Running your Kollekt

Your picture with the gear opens the admin menu. At the top it shows how many members and subscribers you have, and below it:

- **Edit artist page:** your name, bio, cover, socials and links.
- **Stats:** your members, subscribers and what you earn.
- **Groups:** groups of members, for example by country, to send posts to.
- **People:** your member list.
- **Payments:** how your earnings are split. Only the owner manages it.
- **Roles:** who is on your team.

The owner and admins see the admin menu. Moderators reach their tools from the chat.

See [Manage admins and roles](/for-artists/your-space/manage-admins-and-roles) and [Step-by-step: Where do I manage my Kollekt?](https://help.kollekt.io/en/article/where-do-i-manage-my-kollekt-hankhl/)

## Members and subscribers

Everyone who joins is a member, for free. Members read the Direct Line and talk in Main Chat. Subscribers also get your subscriber-only posts and the Subscribers Chat, and can send photos and videos in chat. See [How subscriptions and earnings work](/for-artists/earn/how-subscriptions-and-earnings-work).
