How to tell your existing fanbase you're on Kollekt. What to say, where to post it, and what makes the difference between a few sign-ups and a real first wave.

One announcement rarely reaches your whole audience. Most fans need to see it more than once before they tap through, and when they do, they need a reason: something you'll post on Kollekt that they can't get anywhere else. Voice notes, demos, studio moments, the personal stuff you don't post anywhere else.

## Before you announce

Two things should be true:

- Your Home has a cover photo and your artist name is right. See [Edit your Artist page](/for-artists/home/editing-the-artist-page).
- You've got at least one post in your Direct Line. A photo and a sentence is fine. See [Send a Direct Line message](/for-artists/direct-line/sending-messages) for how.

An empty page is a weak first impression. Worth the extra minute.

## What makes an announcement work

- **Point at a thing, not at Kollekt.** "New voice note on my Kollekt" converts better than "I'm on Kollekt now." A reason to tap beats an announcement every time.
- **Keep it short.** Fans are scrolling. One line gets read.
- **Sound like you.** Write it the way you normally post. Not a polished version of it.

## Where to post it

Three surfaces cover most of your audience.

### Instagram Story (with the link sticker)

The biggest-reach surface for most artists. The link sticker is the only way Instagram lets fans tap through. Use it, and point at whatever you just posted in your Direct Line.

> new voice note on my kollekt → [link sticker]

See [Post your Kollekt link on Instagram Story](/for-artists/bring-fans-in/instagram-stories) for the mechanical how-to.

### Instagram feed post

A short pinned post on your profile. The photo does the work. The caption just tells fans where to go.

> voice notes + demos. on my kollekt. link in bio.

### Close-fan DMs

The highest-converting move, and most artists skip it. Send 1:1 messages to 10–20 people you'd actually text. Not a mass DM, one at a time.

> hey, voice notes + demos from me are going on kollekt now. thought you'd want in. kollekt.io/yourname
