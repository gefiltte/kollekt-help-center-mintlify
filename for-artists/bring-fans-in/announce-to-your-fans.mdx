One announcement rarely reaches everyone. Many people see a post more than once before they tap through, and when they do, it helps to have a reason: something on your Kollekt they can't get anywhere else. Voice notes, demos, studio moments, or the more personal side you don't share elsewhere.

## Before you announce

When the first people arrive, it's nice if there's already something to see:

- A cover photo and your name the way you want them. See [Edit your Artist page](/for-artists/your-space/editing-the-artist-page).
- A post or two on your Direct Line. A photo and a sentence is plenty. See [Send a Direct Line message](/for-artists/your-space/sending-messages).

## What tends to work

- **A reason, not just a link.** "New voice note on my Kollekt" gives people something to tap for, more than "I'm on Kollekt now" does.
- **Short.** People are scrolling. One line often does it.
- **Your own voice.** The way you normally post, not a polished version of it.

## Places to share it

A few places artists often use. Some use all of them, some just one.

### Instagram Story

A story with a link sticker lets people tap straight through. In the Kollekt app, **Share story** on your Kollekt opens Instagram with a story ready to post. Pointing at something new on your Direct Line gives the story a reason.

> new voice note on my kollekt → [link sticker]

See [Post your Kollekt link on Instagram Story](/for-artists/bring-fans-in/instagram-stories).

### A post on your profile

A short post, maybe pinned, stays there for anyone who visits your profile later. The photo does the work, and the caption says where to go.

> voice notes + demos. on my kollekt. link in bio.

### Personal messages

A personal message to people you're close to, one at a time, can feel very different from a public post. For a band, each member might message their own circle.

> hey, I'm putting voice notes and demos on kollekt now. thought you'd want in. kollekt.io/yourname

### At shows

A link on the screen or a quick mention from the stage reaches people who are already there for you. Joining takes a moment on their phone.
