The first announcement is only the start. What happens in the weeks after is what makes a Kollekt feel alive, and every artist finds their own pace for it. Here are some things that tend to help.

## Finding a rhythm

There's no formula. Some ideas artists use:

- **Small, regular posts on the Direct Line.** A photo and a sentence, a voice note, or a quick question like "this one or that one?". Easy often works better than polished.
- **A story when something new is on your Kollekt.** "New voice note on my Kollekt" with a link sticker gives people on Instagram a reason to come over.
- **Dropping into the chat now and then.** A heart or a reply here and there shows members you're there. It doesn't have to take long.
- **Something they don't get elsewhere.** People join for what isn't on your other channels: voice notes, demos, studio moments, the more personal side.
- **Scheduled posts for busy weeks.** A post can be written ahead and go out later, on tour or while you're in the studio.

Members often start to expect a certain rhythm, so steady tends to matter more than a lot.

For a band or a team, sharing the posting between people can make the rhythm easier to keep.

## From your other channels

Many new members keep arriving from Instagram, TikTok and your other channels. Each new post on your Kollekt is something specific to mention there. "Just posted a voice note about [thing] on my Kollekt" says more than "I'm on Kollekt!"

A line in a caption, like "more of this on my Kollekt", is sometimes enough.

## Members bring people too

When members share your Kollekt, they share their own link, and the people they bring in count as their referrals on your leaderboard. Members who like being part of it often become the ones who bring others.

## If things feel quiet

A quiet start is normal. A story only reaches some of your followers, and many people see a mention more than once before they tap through. A few things worth a look:

- **Does the link in your bios open your Kollekt?** A private browser window shows what a visitor sees.
- **Is there something to see when people arrive?** A cover photo, your name and a post or two on the Direct Line.
- **Does the announcement say what people get?** "Voice notes and things I don't post anywhere else" gives more of a reason than "join my Kollekt".
- **Has it been shared in more than one place?** A story, a post, personal messages, a mention at a show.

When you mention Kollekt again, pointing at something specific, like a new track, a voice note, a drop or a show, tends to work better than pointing at Kollekt itself.
