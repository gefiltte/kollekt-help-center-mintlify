title: "Share your Kollekt link"

Your Kollekt has its own link to share anywhere: your bio, stories, posts and shows. Tap the share button on your Home, choose where the link opens, and share it as an Instagram story, copy it, or send it through another app.

For ideas on where to share it, see the other pages in this section.
