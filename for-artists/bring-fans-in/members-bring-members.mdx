Your own posts and stories bring people in. But once a Kollekt gets going, the members themselves often become the best way new people find you. Kollekt has a few things built in that make this visible and a bit of fun: referrals, a leaderboard, badges and member numbers.

## How it fits together

1. **A member shares your Kollekt** with their own link, from the share button on your Home.
2. **A friend opens the link and joins.** Their phone or browser remembers whose link it was for 30 days.
3. **When the friend subscribes or gets a drop for the first time,** it counts as the member's referral.
4. **Referrals count the most on your leaderboard,** so the members who bring others along rise to the top.
5. **Their place on the leaderboard is also their Member #,** which shows next to their name in your chats.

So members who enjoy being part of your Kollekt get recognised for bringing others, and the people they bring in become members who can do the same.

## The leaderboard

Every Kollekt has a leaderboard that ranks members by how they take part. It's at your Kollekt's address with /leaderboard at the end, for example app.kollekt.io/ilona/leaderboard, and anyone with the link can see it. It shows the top 50.

**Overall** puts everything together. Referrals count the most, then spending and how long someone has been subscribed, then messages and streaks, then reactions, votes and time. Overall slowly goes down while someone is away, so taking part now and then counts for more than one busy day.

Separate tabs rank members by one thing at a time: Messages, Reactions, Votes, Referrals, Time, Spend and Streak.

## Badges and member numbers

In your chats, a name can carry a few marks:

- **The subscriber diamond.** Its colour deepens the longer someone stays subscribed: blue in the first month, green in the second, red from the third month on.
- **Your own badges.** You can give a group of members a badge of their own. Groups are chosen by country and join date, so a badge could go to your earliest members or to everyone in one country.
- **A Day One badge.** You can turn it on for your earliest members: either the first members up to a number you choose, or everyone who joined before a date you choose.
- **A shield** for your moderators.

Tapping a name shows two numbers:

- **Member #** is their place among all your members, the same as their Overall place on the leaderboard. It moves as people take part.
- **Subscriber #** is the order they subscribed in. Subscriber #5 was the fifth to subscribe.

## Ideas for using it

Every community is different, so these are just possibilities:

- **Mentioning the leaderboard now and then,** for example in a Direct Line post, so members know it exists.
- **Thanking the people at the top,** like the members who brought in the most friends this month. Some artists reply to them in the chat or send them a voice note.
- **A badge for a special group,** like everyone who joined in the first month, or members in a country you're about to tour.
- **A raffle in a drop** for members who take part, with a meet and greet, tickets or signed merch as the prize.
- **Reminding members how to share,** since the share button on your Home is what gives them their own link.

For a band or a manager, the leaderboard can also help spot the members who might enjoy helping more, for example as moderators.

## Good to know

- **Share from Home.** The share button on your Home gives a personal link. Sharing from a chat's menu sends a plain link, which doesn't count as a referral.
- **Own purchases don't count** as referrals.
- **Ranks update every few minutes.**
- **Members who leave drop off the leaderboard,** and their score is kept in case they come back.
