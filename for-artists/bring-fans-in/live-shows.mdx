At a show, people are already there for you. It's a good moment to tell them where they can stay close after the night is over.

## A QR code

Kollekt doesn't make QR codes itself, but any free QR code generator works. Point it at your link, for example `kollekt.io/ilona`, and the same image can go on screens, signs and print.

Joining is free and works in the phone's browser, no app needed. People enter their email and confirm with a link Kollekt emails them, so some will finish joining after the show. That's fine: the link waits in their inbox.

## Where it can go

Some places artists use. One or two is often enough, and they can change from tour to tour.

**On the screens.** If the venue has screens, the QR can loop between songs with a short line, for example:

- "Voice note from me tomorrow. Scan."
- "The stuff I don't post anywhere else."
- "Scan for tomorrow's setlist, and everything else."

**At the merch table.** A small sign next to the card reader reaches people who already stopped by. A few lines for a sign:

- "Scan for the good stuff first."
- "Where I post voice notes + new music."
- "Unreleased things live here."

**A small card or flyer.** A card with the QR and one line, handed out at the door or left at the merch table:

- "Tonight's setlist, tomorrow morning. Scan."
- "One link for everything from me."

**The tour poster.** A QR in a corner with a short line, like "Find me on Kollekt → [scan]."

## A word from the stage

A mention from the stage can say more than any sign, especially when it gives a reason: a voice note tomorrow, the setlist, something from the rest of the tour. Many artists keep it to one short mention. Some lines to borrow or make your own:

> "Before the last song. If you want voice notes, early demos, the stuff I don't post anywhere else, scan the QR on the screen."

> "Quick thing. Scan the QR before you leave and I'll send a voice note from tomorrow's studio day."

> "If you liked tonight, there's a QR on the screen. That's where I'll post the rest of the tour from. Tonight's setlist, tomorrow's soundcheck, all of it."

> "One more thing. I made a Kollekt. It's where I actually talk to you. Scan if you want in."

If you promise something from the stage, like a voice note the next morning, it's nice when it's waiting on your Direct Line when people arrive.
