## Where to add your link

Your link is always `kollekt.io/yourname`. Add it wherever fans can tap on your profile:

- **Instagram.** In your bio link.
- **TikTok.** In your bio link.
- **YouTube.** On the channel About page, in a pinned comment under each video, and at the top of every video description.
- **X (Twitter).** In your bio link.
- **Facebook (artist page).** As your Website link.
- **Twitch.** In your Panels or profile links, so viewers can find it during the stream.

## What to write next to the link

A short, specific line next to the link works much better than a generic "music + more". A few that work on Instagram, TikTok, YouTube, or anywhere else you have a bio:

- **"voice notes + demos ↓"**
- **"Where I post the stuff I don't post anywhere else"**
- **"direct line from me → kollekt"**
- **"Demos, voice notes, tour updates"**
- **"everything else lives here ↓"**

Each line above tells fans what they'll find. A bio like "now on Kollekt" doesn't say much. It just announces. Tell fans what's actually there.

## Tips

- **Use the same label everywhere.** `Kollekt` or `@yourname on Kollekt`. Fans see it a few times and it starts to mean something.
- **If you only have time for one today, do the platform you post on most.** Start where your audience already is.
- **On Instagram and TikTok, pin a post or video that explains what fans will find on your Kollekt.** Pair it with the bio link.
