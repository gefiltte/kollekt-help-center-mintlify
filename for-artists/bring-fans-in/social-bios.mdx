A post is gone from the feed in a day or two, but a link in your bio stays. Anyone who visits your profile, whether they found you today or a year ago, can tap through to your Kollekt.

## Your link

Your link is kollekt.io followed by your Kollekt's name, for example `kollekt.io/ilona`. You can also copy it from **Share** on your Kollekt. See [Share your Kollekt link](/for-artists/bring-fans-in/sharing-your-page).

## Where it can go

Most platforms have a spot for a link on your profile:

- **Instagram** and **TikTok:** the link in your bio.
- **YouTube:** your channel's links, and video descriptions or a pinned comment if you like.
- **X:** the website field in your profile.
- **Facebook (artist page):** the Website field.
- **Twitch:** your panels or profile links, so viewers can find it during a stream.

If you're short on time, the platform where your audience already is makes a good start. For a band, the band's own profiles and each member's personal profile can all point to the same Kollekt.

## A line next to the link

A short line that says what's there gives people a reason to tap. Some ideas to borrow or change:

- "voice notes + demos ↓"
- "where I post the stuff I don't post anywhere else"
- "direct line from me → kollekt"
- "demos, voice notes, tour updates"
- "everything else lives here ↓"

"Now on Kollekt" works too, but a line about what people will find usually says more.

## A few more ideas

- **The same label everywhere,** like "Kollekt" or "@yourname on Kollekt", so it becomes familiar.
- **A pinned post or video** that shows what's on your Kollekt, alongside the bio link.
