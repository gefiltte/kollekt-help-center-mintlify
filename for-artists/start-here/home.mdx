Home is your public Kollekt page at `kollekt.io/yourname`. Anyone can see it, even without joining. It's the page fans see when they tap any link you share. Cover photo, name, social icons, and link groups all live here.

Inside the app, Home is also where you start. From here you go to [Direct Line](/for-artists/start-here/direct-line), [Chat](/for-artists/start-here/chat), and your admin tools.

![Artist Home page on Kollekt](screenshots/surface-home.png)

## Set it up

- [Edit your Artist page](/for-artists/home/editing-the-artist-page). Update your cover photo, name, and socials.
- [Add and organize link groups](/for-artists/home/add-and-organize-link-groups). Bundle related links into sections.
- [Your Artist page, explained](/for-artists/home/artist-home). The full walkthrough.
