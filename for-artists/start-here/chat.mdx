Chat is the group room inside your Kollekt space, where fans talk to each other and to you. They can post, react, reply, and thread on every message. Open to all members by default; subscribers also get their own private room.

It's where loyalty compounds. When fans start talking to other fans who care about you, the space becomes sticky in a way a broadcast feed never is.

![Chat with active community conversation](screenshots/surface-chat.png)

## Use it

- [Run your community chat](/for-artists/chat/community-chat). Switch rooms, post as artist or personal.
- [Moderate your chat](/for-artists/chat/moderate-your-chat). Timeouts, promote moderators.
