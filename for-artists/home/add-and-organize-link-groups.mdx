A link group is a section on your Artist page that bundles related links. Like a "Songs" section for your newest songs, or a "Tickets" section for your gigs. Add as many as you need.

## Create a link group

You add link groups from the editor. Tap the settings icon at the bottom right of your Artist page, choose **Edit Artist Page**, then scroll to the bottom and tap **+ New link group**.

1. Tap the settings icon at the bottom right of your Artist page.
2. Tap **Edit Artist Page**.
3. Scroll to the bottom and tap **+ New link group**.
4. Give the group a title fans will recognize. Examples: **Merch**, **New songs**, **Tickets**, **Socials**.
5. Add one or more links. Each link gets a title, a URL, and an optional image.
6. Tap **Save** in the top right.

![New link group with a link being added](screenshots/home-edit-link-filled.png)

## Add, reorder, or remove links

Inside an existing group, you can:

- Add another link.
- Reorder links by dragging.
- Remove a single link.
- Reorder whole groups on the page.
- Delete a group entirely.

![Editor with multiple link groups expanded](screenshots/home-edit-link-groups-expanded.png)

## What fans see

Each group becomes its own section on your public page, with the links inside it.

![Artist page with link groups visible to fans](screenshots/home-artist-page-with-link-groups.png)

## Tips

- **2 to 4 groups is the sweet spot.** More than that and the page starts to feel like a list.
- **Use images when you can.** Thumbnail links work better than plain text rows.
- **Name groups by category, not by vibe.** "New music" beats "What I'm into". Fans scan, they don't read.
