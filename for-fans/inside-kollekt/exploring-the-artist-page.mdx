Kollekt is where artists keep the people who care most about their music close. Each artist has their own Kollekt: their news and links, the posts they send to everyone who joined, and the chats where members talk. Joining is free, and it works in your phone's browser as well as in the app.

## Inside an artist's Kollekt

You move around an artist's Kollekt with the tab bar at the bottom of the screen.

| Tab | What's there |
| --- | --- |
| Home | The artist's picture, name and bio, their social links, and the links they're sharing right now, like new music or show tickets. |
| Drops | Things the artist offers, like a demo, a presale or merch. Some are free, some have a price. The tab shows while the artist has a drop out. |
| Direct Line | Posts from the artist to everyone who joined: words, photos, videos, voice notes and links. |
| Chats | Where members talk with each other, and where the artist sometimes drops in. |

When the artist posts on the Direct Line, you hear about it by push notification, or by email if push can't reach you.

## Members and subscribers

Everyone who joins is a **member**, for free. Members read the Direct Line and talk in Main Chat.

**Subscribers** support the artist every month, and get a little more in return: the posts the artist keeps for subscribers, the Subscribers Chat, photos and videos in chat, and a highlighted name with a badge that grows the longer they stay. Subscribing is always optional. See [Subscribing to an artist](/for-fans/subscribing/subscribing-to-an-artist).

## More than one artist

You can join as many artists as you like, and each membership is separate. Your picture at the top right opens the menu, where the artists you've joined are listed under **Artists** and you can search for more. If an artist isn't on Kollekt yet, you can join their list and hear when they arrive.

[Step-by-step: How do I find more artists?](https://help.kollekt.io/en/article/how-do-i-find-more-artists-2m538z/)

## Bringing friends along

The share button on an artist's Home gives you your own link to their Kollekt, to send to a friend or share in a story. When someone you bring in subscribes or gets a drop, it counts as your referral on the artist's leaderboard.

[Step-by-step: How do I share an artist with friends?](https://help.kollekt.io/en/article/how-do-i-share-an-artist-1t0qzq9/)

## Leaving an artist

The three dots at the top right of an artist's Home let you unfollow them. You can always come back by finding them again.
